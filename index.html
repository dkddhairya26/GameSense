TRANSPARENCY

Actual data coverage & limitations

Soccer

OpenFootball CC0 completed club results, observed 2010–11 to 2026–27. No individual footballer statistics, lineups or xG.

NBA

BoxScore Lab CC BY 4.0 player-season totals, observed 2022–23 to 2026–27. No complete earlier careers. Some historical metrics missing.

AFL

Desktop project has an optional AFL player-game research provider, but its third-party AFL Tables-derived records are not bundled or redistributed here. Use a permitted CSV/JSON import for real player stats. No fabricated AFL rows.

AFL player source (rights review required) · Squiggle team matches (separate source)

Formula 1

F1DB CC BY 4.0 driver race results, observed 1950–2026. Historic qualifying and telemetry are incomplete. Points totals are observed records, not guaranteed official championship totals.
