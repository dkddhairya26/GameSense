ALGORITHMS & DATA QUALITY

How the analysis works

Checking loaded records…

Soccer points: calculated as 3 per win, 1 per draw and 0 per loss, rather than trusting an ambiguous source points field.

GameSense performance index: a transparent sport-specific experimental score out of 100. Scores are neither official ratings nor comparable between sports.

Select a sport to see its formula.

Processing: fetch → normalize → validate → deduplicate → filter → aggregate → rank → visualize.

Limits: historical fields and season coverage vary. Cricket is a curated leading-performer selection; NBA rows are player-season totals; F1 and Soccer results may be event-level.

SOURCE LEDGER

What the data can tell us

Soccer

Bundled club-match results from OpenFootball. Team-level historical records only; no player-level football statistics bundled.

NBA

Bundled player-season totals from the supplied GameSense archive. Coverage starts from the observed seasons bundled in that archive.

Cricket — IPL & International ODIs

208 curated IPL player-season records across 19 observed seasons, 2007/08–2026, plus international ODI, T20I and Test match results, 2001–2025, sourced from Cricsheet-derived CSV archives. Selection: the seven highest-run and four highest-wicket players per season with duplicates removed. This is a curated sample, not complete IPL coverage.

Sources: Cricsheet · IPL ETL derived data · ODC-BY 1.0 license.

Formula 1

Bundled F1 driver race results from the supplied GameSense archive. Historical telemetry is not bundled.

GameSense is a student project and not affiliated with any sporting league. The site prioritises honest source coverage over pretending to have every statistic ever recorded.
